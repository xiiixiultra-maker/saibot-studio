The mango

The dye only touches the off-white upper. The midsole keeps the production fade it ships with, because acid dye bonds to nylon and the sole unit resists it.

And the upper is cream, not white, so the dye tints it rather than covering it. Every colour comes off warmer than the bath looks: greens land olive, reds land terracotta. The swatches here are the on-cream values, what the pair actually ends up.
Heads up. Shoe art is rendered from the confidential FW26 CAD and the colourways here are recoloured samples, not approved product. The live creator page carries no product imagery at all. Dye direction is still an open question, see the spec.
