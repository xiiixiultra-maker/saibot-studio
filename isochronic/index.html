About the studio

  
How it works. An isochronic tone is a steady tone switched on and off at a set rate. Your brain tends to follow strong rhythms, and that is the idea behind entrainment. Binaural beats play a slightly different tone in each ear and let your brain hear the difference as a beat, so they need headphones.

  
What the science says. Research on brainwave entrainment is mixed. Some studies find small effects on relaxation and attention and many find none, so treat these as sound that may suit you rather than medicine. The breath pacer is the most grounded part: slow breathing around six breaths a minute is well supported for calming the body.

  
Frequencies. Delta is under 4 Hz, theta 4 to 8, alpha 8 to 13, beta 13 to 30 and gamma above 30. 432 Hz and solfeggio carriers are an aesthetic choice. They change how it sounds, not what it does.

  
Safety. Rhythmic stimulation can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive or other seizure conditions, so skip it if that is you. Never listen while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention. Keep the volume low, especially on headphones.

  
Keys. Space plays and pauses. F opens immersive mode. The arrow keys jump through the journey, or change the rate in free play. Delete removes the selected point on the journey.
